# Implementation status

This file tracks shipped functionality against `MASTER_PRD.md`. “Available” means the current application contains a working UI/API/database path for the listed actions; it does not mean the full PRD module is complete or production-ready.

## Available in the current increment

- Foundation: company setup, owner login, opaque authenticated sessions, owner role/permissions, and audit log baseline.
- Masters: company-scoped create/list/search and activate/deactivate for customers, suppliers, brokers, products, and chambers. Master creation and status changes are audited and committed with their audit records.
- Production: create batch, start planned batch, enter good/second/broken/rejected output, complete once, and atomically add good/second output to stock movements with an audit event.
- Stock: read current location/product/quality balances from the movement ledger; view chronological movements with before/after quantity, user, reason and reference; create additional stock locations; post permission-controlled, reason-required increases/decreases; block reductions beyond stock at the selected location; reclassify good/second stock into non-saleable damaged stock atomically; transfer stock between active locations with paired, audited stock-out/stock-in movements and source-balance protection. Physical stocktakes snapshot all active product qualities at a location, require an entered count for every line, submit for review, and post audited variance movements only on approval. Approval is blocked if movements changed after the count snapshot. Each active company receives a Main yard location and production/purchase/sales movements are assigned there.
- Sales: create a single-product order from a customer/product master, calculate rates and tax on the API, issue an invoice once when sufficient stock exists, post stock out plus balanced receivable/sales/tax journal lines, receive invoice payments, and inspect customer balances/ledger. Controlled partial/full returns create numbered credit notes, reverse output tax and receivable, route returned items to good/second/damaged stock, enforce remaining quantities and receipt caps, and record audit history atomically. Number sequences and audit rows are transaction-backed. Credit notes can be applied to another posted invoice for the same customer or refunded through cash/bank with per-note available-credit and invoice-balance caps, balanced journals, audit records, Tamil/English controls, and invoice balance updates.
- Purchases: create a supplier order using product purchase-rate/tax defaults; record separate partial/full goods receipts that post inbound good stock and inventory/Goods-Received-Not-Invoiced journals; post one or more supplier bills only against received and not-yet-billed quantities, reclassifying interim liability to input tax/payables; retain receipt, bill and audit history; post bill-linked supplier returns capped by billed quantity and saleable stock; reverse input tax/payables, issue supplier credit and post stock out with audit; record full or partial supplier payments and inspect bill balances and supplier payable ledgers. Receipt quantities cannot exceed ordered quantities, and supplier/bill references are company-scoped.
- People and attendance: create and edit company-scoped employee records with before/after audit history; activate/deactivate employees; record or revise one attendance status per employee/date with overtime and notes; issue cash/bank advances as employee receivables; record capped partial repayments; view employee advance balances, attendance and posted advance journals. Payroll calculation and salary payment remain disabled pending owner/accountant pay-rule and statutory configuration.
- Expenses: create company-scoped operating expense records from seeded categories with date, amount, payment method, description and reference; atomically debit the category expense account, credit cash/bank, and audit the posting. List records with their journal entry number. English/Tamil page and dashboard entry are included.
- Accounting reports: date-filtered trial balance and profit-and-loss summaries derived from journal lines. Trial-balance debit/credit totals are checked at runtime; journals retain both posting timestamp and effective transaction date so report filters follow the source invoice/receipt/bill/payment/expense date. The P&L explicitly excludes inventory COGS until valuation is implemented.
- Loading and delivery: create one loading job per invoice, record vehicle/driver/destination and server start/end time, upload immutable before/during/after photos, record actual and independent verified quantities, block mismatches until reasoned permission-checked approval, dispatch and mark delivered with server times.
- UI: English/Tamil switching persists locally. The People and attendance area is available from desktop sidebar and dashboard, and from a dashboard module on mobile. Master, production, people/attendance, sales, loading and purchases screens use touch-sized controls and horizontally scrollable data tables on small screens. A persistent mobile bottom nav links overview, masters, production, sales, purchases and delivery; production exposes stock balances, adjustments, location creation and transfers. Purchases provides order, supplier-bill/payment and supplier-ledger views, with dashboard and navigation entry points.

## Still required by the PRD

Administration beyond initial owner setup; branch and financial-year workflows; labour piece-rate rules, salary calculation/payments/deductions, vehicle and driver masters, and tax/unit masters; location editing/deactivation and branch-specific stock rules; batch milestones, costs and capacity rules; stock opening balances as a distinct transaction and stock returns; sales invoice reversals, credit controls, customer-specific rates, shipment-linked stock timing, broker commissions and collection allocations; inventory valuation/COGS; supplier statutory tax compliance; expense category management and approval for large expenses; chart-of-accounts management, cash/bank books and closing, balance sheet and reconciled financial statements; approval workflows; richer reporting/export; owner mobile and offline sync; notifications; auditor center; integrations; restore/security/production deployment checks; and full UAT/reconciliation.

Tax treatment, commission policies, rates, payroll rules and accounting decisions remain configurable decisions for the business owner/accountant. No release should be represented as complete until critical PRD workflows and reconciliations pass and receive UAT sign-off.
