# Database baseline and master-data increment

PostgreSQL is the system of record. The API resolves the active company from the authenticated session and includes it in every master-data read or write. The browser never connects directly to PostgreSQL.

## Applied schema

- `companies`, `branches`, `financial_years`, `users`, roles, permissions, sessions, company settings, numbering sequences and audit logs are created by `001_foundation.sql`.
- `business_partners` stores customers, suppliers and brokers, separated by a constrained `partner_type`. Codes are unique per company and partner type. Optional phone, email, address, GSTIN and configurable credit days are stored with the record.
- `products` stores company-scoped product codes, units and non-negative indicative selling rates. Rates are master defaults only; sales workflows must snapshot applicable rates and calculate invoice totals server-side.
- `chambers` stores company-scoped chamber codes, capacities and capacity units. Production records and stock movements are future increments.
- Master creation and activation changes are committed atomically with audit events. Records are deactivated rather than deleted.
- `production_batches` ties each batch to a company-owned chamber and product, stores planned output and the four quality quantities, and enforces a one-way planned/running/completed/cancelled status.
- `stock_movements` is an append-only movement ledger. Completing a batch stores good and second-quality quantities as inbound stock movements in the same transaction as batch completion and its audit event. Broken and rejected quantities remain recorded on the batch but are not saleable inventory. Current balances are calculated from movements rather than edited balance fields.
- `stock_locations` provides a company-scoped location dimension; migration seeds `MAIN` and backfills stock movements. New production, purchase and sales movements post to this location. Owners can create additional coded locations. The stock-adjustment API writes reasoned inventory gains/losses only after locking the product and validating location availability; stock cannot go negative at a location. Damage is a paired outbound good/second movement and inbound damaged-quality movement in one transaction. `stock_transfers` records between-location transfer headers while two paired stock movements preserve source and destination history; product locking and source balance checks prevent over-transfer. The ledger derives before/after quantities and retains actor, reason and reference.
- `stocktakes` and `stocktake_lines` capture per-location expected balances for all active product qualities, physical counts, and approval state. Counts cannot be submitted until each item is counted. Approving a submitted count locks affected products and verifies that current quantities still match the snapshot; changed inventory forces a fresh count. Approved variances post append-only stock adjustments with references and audit history; rejected counts post no inventory movement. Separate opening balances, stock valuation adjustments and stock-return workflows remain outstanding.
- `sales_orders` and `sales_order_lines` snapshot the active product rate and configured product tax rate at order time. An order can be invoiced once.
- Posting an invoice locks its order and product row, checks saleable stock, writes immutable invoice/line records, posts outbound quantity, and writes balanced accounts-receivable, sales, and output-tax journal lines with audit history in one database transaction. Invoice totals are calculated by the API. Product tax is configured as an exclusive percentage on the product master and must be verified by the business accountant/CA before statutory use.
- Customer receipts are stored as transaction rows. Invoice allocations cannot exceed the remaining invoice balance. A receipt posts a cash/bank debit and receivable credit in the same journal transaction; customer balances are derived from the receivable account lines, not editable balance fields. A receipt with no invoice can be recorded as on-account through the API, but the current UI only records invoice-linked receipts.
- `sales_returns` and `sales_return_lines` retain numbered, reasoned credit notes linked to posted invoices and original invoice lines. Each return locks its invoice lines and product, caps cumulative quantities to the sold quantity, records the returned condition, and posts good/second/damaged stock plus a balanced journal reversing sales (contra-income 4200), output tax and customer receivable in one audited transaction. Invoice balance and returnable quantities are derived from receipts and returns; a negative invoice balance is presented as customer credit, and receipt posting is capped after credits. Invoice cancellation/reversal and allocation of unrelated on-account receipts remain future work.
- `products.purchase_rate` and `purchase_tax_rate` hold configurable defaults. Purchase orders snapshot rate and tax. Separate partial/full `purchase_receipts` lock the purchase line/product, cap received quantities at the ordered amount, post inbound good-stock movement, and debit inventory against the supplier-specific goods-received-not-invoiced liability (account 2300). Bills can cover only received/unbilled quantities; posting debits account 2300 and input tax, credits accounts payable, and records an audited bill. Multiple bills may follow multiple receipts. Receipt and bill dates and references remain distinct.
- `purchase_returns` and `purchase_return_lines` retain reasoned supplier credit notes linked to bill lines. Quantities cannot exceed the remaining billed line or available good stock in the main yard. A return posts a good-quality stock-out, debit to accounts payable (supplier credit), credit to inventory and input tax, and audit history atomically. Returns against settled bills remain supplier credits in the ledger; cash refunds and allocating supplier credits to later bills remain future work.
- Supplier payments may be bill-linked or on-account. Bill-linked payments are serialized against the bill and capped at the remaining balance; supplier identity is constrained to match the selected bill. Payment posts accounts-payable debit and cash/bank credit together with an audit event. Supplier bill balances and supplier ledgers are derived from transaction rows and payable journal entries.
- `expense_categories` maps active company categories to a chart-of-accounts code. `expenses` holds the date, amount, payment method, description/reference, and optional branch/chamber/batch/vehicle/employee references. Posting an expense writes the expense debit and cash/bank credit in a balanced journal transaction together with its audit event; the API does not support silent edits/deletion.
- `journal_entries.effective_date` stores the business date of the originating invoice, receipt, supplier bill/payment or expense, separately from the server posting timestamp. Financial report period filters use effective date; historical rows are backfilled from their original posting date.
- Read-only `accounting/trial-balance` and `accounting/profit-loss` endpoints aggregate posted journal lines by date range. The current P&L excludes inventory cost of goods sold and accruals that have not been posted.
- The current sales stock posting reduces saleable quantities at invoice time. Inventory value/COGS posting and costing are not yet implemented; this increment must not be treated as complete financial statements.
- `loading_sessions` links one loading record to an invoice, stores vehicle/driver/destination, server timestamps, invoiced/loaded/verified quantities and status. Verification compares invoice quantity, actual loaded quantity and independent verified quantity against company `loading_mismatch_tolerance`. An out-of-tolerance mismatch blocks dispatch until a user with `loading.approve` enters an audited reason. Dispatch and delivered timestamps are append-only state transitions.
- `loading_photos` stores before/during/after JPEG, PNG or WebP images as protected database bytea, capped at 750 KB each. The endpoint validates file signatures, requires authentication/permission, records server timestamp/uploader, and offers no silent replacement or deletion. Move photo storage to private object storage before production scale; browser upload currently needs a live connection.

- `employees`, `attendance_records` and `employee_advances` track personnel, daily attendance and advances. Employee master edits lock the row and write before/after audit data; status changes are audited. Attendance is unique per employee/day and can be corrected through an audited upsert. Employee advances debit the employee-specific receivable (account 1400), credit cash/bank, and retain journal/ledger links. Repayments are capped at the employee receivable balance and credit account 1400 against cash/bank. Payroll is deliberately not calculated until pay rules and statutory settings are confirmed.

## Migration

`002_core_masters.sql` adds the core tables and master permissions. `003_production_stock.sql` adds batches, stock movements and production/stock permissions. `004_sales_ledger.sql` adds sales, receivables and journal structures plus baseline accounts/sequences. `005_loading_delivery.sql` adds dispatch workflow/evidence and tolerance configuration. `006_loading_variance.sql` stores loaded-vs-planned and verified-vs-loaded variances independently. `007_purchases_payables.sql` adds purchase defaults, supplier ledger dimensions, orders, bills, payment records, permissions, accounts and sequences. `008_purchase_order_integrity.sql` constrains each bill to the supplier on its source order. `009_expenses.sql` adds expense categories, expense entries, their account mappings, permissions and numbering. `010_journal_effective_date.sql` adds business dates to accounting entries and backfills existing rows. `011_stock_locations_adjustments.sql` seeds a main stock location, backfills stock movements, adds damaged quality and stock adjustment permissions. `012_stock_transfers_locations.sql` adds audited transfer headers, transfer movement type and location/transfer permissions. `013_people_attendance_advances.sql` adds employee, attendance and advance ledgers and their permissions/account sequence. `014_employee_edit_permission.sql` adds the employee status permission. `015_employee_advance_repayments.sql` adds capped, journal-backed repayments. `016_sales_returns.sql` adds invoice-linked sales credit notes, return permissions and return numbering. `017_sales_returns_income_account.sql` classifies the sales-returns contra account as income so the P&L nets returns against sales. `018_stocktakes.sql` adds physical count snapshots, variance review permissions and stocktake numbering. `019_separate_purchase_receipts.sql` splits goods receipts from supplier bills, permits partial receipts and billing, adds the GRNI liability account and receipt permission/numbering. `020_purchase_returns.sql` adds bill-line supplier returns, stock-out movement type, return permission and credit-note numbering. Migrations grant new permissions to existing system-owner roles. Apply with `npm run db:migrate`; migrations are checksummed and must not be edited after application.

Migration `021_customer_credit_settlement.sql` adds customer credit-note allocations to posted invoices, cash/bank refunds, the settlement permission, tenant-scoped document references, and indexed lookups for used and remaining credit.

## Remaining schema work

The PRD also requires employee/driver/vehicle masters, production milestones and cost layers, stock opening balances as distinct transactions and stock returns, sales invoice reversals and shipment-linked stock timing, customer-specific pricing/credit limits, broker commissions, supplier statutory tax compliance, richer driver delivery proof/signature/OTP and object storage, payroll, inventory valuation/COGS, chart-of-accounts management, cash/bank closing, approval workflows, offline/idempotent sync, and reconciliation/read models. These must be added as separate increments before the corresponding workflows are represented as production-ready.
