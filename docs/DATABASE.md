# Database baseline and master-data increment

PostgreSQL is the system of record. The API resolves the active company from the authenticated session and includes it in every master-data read or write. The browser never connects directly to PostgreSQL.

## Applied schema

- `companies`, `branches`, `financial_years`, `users`, roles, permissions, sessions, company settings, numbering sequences and audit logs are created by `001_foundation.sql`. Migration `033_financial_year_management.sql` enforces non-overlapping date ranges, grants owner calendar access, and seeds the active India April–March year for companies that lack one. Migration `035_financial_year_closures.sql` assigns each journal entry to its effective-date year, blocks postings to closed years, and implements the audited annual P&L close. Year-end close stores an immutable opening-balance snapshot for the next year, including retained earnings after the closing journal. Owners can capture a reasoned baseline for a first or legacy year after the preceding year is closed. Selected-year trial balances start from that snapshot plus current-year journal movements; P&L uses the selected dates.
- `business_partners` stores customers, suppliers and brokers, separated by a constrained `partner_type`. Codes are unique per company and partner type. Optional phone, email, address, GSTIN and configurable credit days are stored with the record.
- `products` stores company-scoped product codes, units and non-negative indicative selling rates. Rates are master defaults only; sales workflows must snapshot applicable rates and calculate invoice totals server-side.
- `chambers` stores company-scoped chamber codes, capacities and capacity units. Production records and stock movements are future increments.
- `vehicles` and `drivers` store tenant-scoped transport masters. Vehicle registration codes and driver licence numbers are unique within a company; optional vehicle capacity/type and driver licence class/phone are searchable and audited. `loading_sessions` holds composite tenant foreign keys to these records while preserving registration, driver name and phone snapshots for historical records. Deactivating a master prevents new loading assignments but leaves past delivery details intact.
- Master creation and activation changes are committed atomically with audit events. Records are deactivated rather than deleted.
- `production_batches` ties each batch to a company-owned chamber and product, stores planned and loaded quantities as base-unit values, and (migration 058) preserves entered good/second/broken/rejected quantities, output unit and conversion factor while storing the four output quantities as base units; it derives process loss, and enforces the ordered planned/loading/firing/cooling/unloading/completed lifecycle. Each transition records a timestamp, actor, quantity when applicable and notes in `production_batch_milestones`, with audit events. Completion is rejected before unloading or when total output exceeds loaded quantity. Historical batches retain their status and receive only the milestone events that can be reconstructed from prior timestamps; missing historical stage times are not fabricated. Its overhead method and rate are snapshotted at creation. `production_cost_lines` records dated, categorized direct costs with the actor, reference and audit event; summary endpoints calculate direct cost, allocated overhead, total batch cost and cost per saleable brick. Overhead may be disabled, set as a percentage of direct costs, or fixed per batch. Direct batch costs post to Production WIP (1350) against a selected cash, bank or payable account. Completion transfers direct cost and snapshotted overhead into saleable FIFO inventory layers and debits Inventory (1300), clears WIP, and reclassifies allocated overhead against Operating Expenses (5100); confirm the overhead policy with the business accountant. Historical cost-layer backfill and period reconciliation remain outstanding.
- `stock_movements` is an append-only movement ledger. Migration 057 adds optional entered-quantity/unit/conversion snapshots for unit-aware manual adjustments; movement `quantity` remains the converted base-unit amount. Completing a batch stores good and second-quality base quantities as inbound stock movements in the same transaction as batch completion and its audit event; output movements retain the entered output quantity, unit and conversion factor. Broken and rejected quantities remain recorded on the batch but are not saleable inventory. Current balances are calculated from movements rather than edited balance fields.
- `stock_locations` provides a company-scoped location dimension; migration seeds `MAIN` and backfills stock movements. New production, purchase and sales movements post to this location. Owners can create additional coded locations. The stock-adjustment API accepts an active configured product unit, converts the entered quantity into base units, and writes reasoned inventory gains/losses only after locking the product and validating location availability; migration 057 preserves entered quantity, unit and conversion factor on manual adjustment movements, stock cannot go negative at a location. Damage is a paired outbound good/second movement and inbound damaged-quality movement in one transaction. `stock_transfers` records between-location transfer headers while two paired stock movements preserve source and destination history; product locking and source balance checks prevent over-transfer. The ledger derives before/after quantities and retains actor, reason and reference.
- `stocktakes` and `stocktake_lines` capture per-location expected balances in base units for all active product qualities, physical counts, and approval state. Migration 057 preserves the entered count, unit and conversion factor while `counted_quantity` contains the base-unit amount. Counts cannot be submitted until each item is counted. Approving a submitted count locks affected products and verifies that current quantities still match the snapshot; changed inventory forces a fresh count. Approved variances post append-only stock adjustments with references and audit history; rejected counts post no inventory movement. Each active location can also receive one owner-authorized, dated opening quantity transaction with separate good/second lines and opening-in movements. Stock valuation adjustments and general stock-return workflows remain outstanding.
- `sales_orders` and `sales_order_lines` snapshot the active product rate and configured product tax rate at order time. An order can be invoiced once.
- Posting an invoice locks its order and product row, checks saleable stock, writes immutable invoice/line records, posts outbound quantity, and writes balanced accounts-receivable, sales, and output-tax journal lines with audit history in one database transaction. Invoice totals are calculated by the API. Product tax is configured as an exclusive percentage on the product master and must be verified by the business accountant/CA before statutory use.
- Customer receipts are stored as transaction rows. Invoice allocations cannot exceed the remaining invoice balance. A receipt posts a cash/bank debit and receivable credit in the same journal transaction; customer balances are derived from the receivable account lines, not editable balance fields. An on-account receipt can be recorded and partially allocated later to matching posted invoices; allocations are audited and capped against both the receipt remainder and invoice balance.
- `sales_returns` and `sales_return_lines` retain numbered, reasoned credit notes linked to posted invoices and original invoice lines. Each return locks its invoice lines and product, caps cumulative quantities to the sold quantity, records the returned condition, and posts good/second/damaged stock plus a balanced journal reversing sales (contra-income 4200), output tax and customer receivable in one audited transaction. Invoice balance and returnable quantities are derived from receipts and returns; a negative invoice balance is presented as customer credit, and receipt posting is capped after credits. Invoice cancellation/reversal remains future work.
- `products.purchase_rate` and `purchase_tax_rate` hold configurable defaults. Purchase orders snapshot rate and tax. Separate partial/full `purchase_receipts` lock the purchase line/product, cap received quantities at the ordered amount, post inbound good-stock movement, and debit inventory against the supplier-specific goods-received-not-invoiced liability (account 2300). Bills can cover only received/unbilled quantities; posting debits account 2300 and input tax, credits accounts payable, and records an audited bill. Multiple bills may follow multiple receipts. Receipt and bill dates and references remain distinct.
- `purchase_returns` and `purchase_return_lines` retain reasoned supplier credit notes linked to bill lines. Return entry accepts an active configured product unit (or the bill’s saved order-unit snapshot), converts to base quantity before billed-remainder, stock, cost and financial calculations, and migration 059 preserves entered quantity/unit/factor while `quantity` stays in base units. Quantities cannot exceed the remaining billed line or available good stock in the main yard. A return posts a good-quality stock-out, debit to accounts payable (supplier credit), credit to inventory and input tax, and audit history atomically. Migration 049 adds audited allocations of unused supplier credit to another open bill for the same supplier. Bill balances and payment caps include allocated credits; unapplied credits remain visible in the purchase workspace. Cash refunds remain future work.
- Supplier payments may be bill-linked or on-account. Bill-linked payments are serialized against the bill and capped at the remaining balance; supplier identity is constrained to match the selected bill. Payment posts accounts-payable debit and cash/bank credit together with an audit event. Supplier bill balances and supplier ledgers are derived from transaction rows and payable journal entries.
- `expense_categories` maps company categories to a chart-of-accounts code. Authorized expense managers can create categories and change their name/active status; categories and posted financial rows are retained rather than deleted. `expenses` holds date, amount, payment method, description/reference, optional operational links, submit/approval status, and decision actor/time/reason. Migration `040_expense_approvals.sql` adds approval state and owner grants. Companies may configure a `large_expense_threshold` setting; it is disabled until configured, and expenses above it remain pending with no journal effect. Approval locks the expense and atomically creates the balanced expense/cash-or-bank journal, changes the state to posted, and records the approver's reason. Rejection records a reason without journal posting. Existing records are backfilled as posted and their existing journals are untouched.
- `journal_entries.effective_date` stores the business date of the originating invoice, receipt, supplier bill/payment or expense, separately from the server posting timestamp. Financial report period filters use effective date; historical rows are backfilled from their original posting date.
- Read-only `accounting/trial-balance` and `accounting/profit-loss` endpoints aggregate posted journal lines by date range. The current P&L excludes inventory cost of goods sold and accruals that have not been posted.
- Sales invoice posting reduces saleable quantities and consumes FIFO inventory layers, posting cost of goods sold to account 5200 and inventory credits to account 1300. Historical FIFO reconstruction and full inventory balance reconciliation remain incomplete.
- `loading_sessions` links one loading record to an invoice and required active company vehicle and driver, stores transport snapshots plus destination, server timestamps, invoiced/loaded/verified quantities and status. Migration 056 snapshots each invoice line's entered product unit and conversion factor; planned, loaded and independently verified quantities retain their entered value/unit/factor while operational quantity and variance fields remain in base units. Verification compares invoice quantity, actual loaded quantity and independent verified quantity against company `loading_mismatch_tolerance`. An out-of-tolerance mismatch blocks dispatch until a user with `loading.approve` enters an audited reason. Dispatch and delivered timestamps are append-only state transitions.
- `loading_photos` stores before/during/after JPEG, PNG or WebP images as protected database bytea, capped at 750 KB each. The endpoint validates file signatures, requires authentication/permission, records server timestamp/uploader, and offers no silent replacement or deletion. Move photo storage to private object storage before production scale; browser upload currently needs a live connection.

- `employees`, `attendance_records` and `employee_advances` track personnel, daily attendance and advances. Employee master edits lock the row and write before/after audit data; status changes are audited. Attendance is unique per employee/day and can be corrected through an audited upsert. Employee advances debit the employee-specific receivable (account 1400), credit cash/bank, and retain journal/ledger links. Repayments are capped at the employee receivable balance and credit account 1400 against cash/bank. Payroll is deliberately not calculated until pay rules and statutory settings are confirmed.

`partner_opening_balances` allows one dated opening position per customer or supplier. Debit and credit positions map to the appropriate receivable/payable control account and opening-balance equity account (3000), with a balanced journal, effective date, reason, permission check and audit record. Customer and supplier ledgers therefore include opening transactions alongside later receipts, invoices, bills and payments. The balance-sheet endpoint aggregates all posted account balances through a selected effective date, presents assets/liabilities/equity and current earnings, and checks that the two sides match. The cash/bank book derives period opening balance, journal-backed movements, running transaction balances and closing balance from cash (1110) or bank/UPI (1120) journal lines. `cash_closings` records one sequential daily count, compares it against the cash book and requires a reason for a variance. Approval posts the balanced shortage/overage journal, records approver and explanation, and prevents later cash journal entries from being backdated into a closed day. `bank_opening_balances` records one dated opening amount to the bank ledger. `bank_reconciliations` and `bank_reconciliation_lines` store the imported statement period and lines, unique journal-line matches, actor/timestamp match history, and reconciled status. Matching requires equal signed amounts and a bank journal line in the period; completion requires every statement row matched and the adjusted posted bank balance to equal the statement closing. The CSV UI currently expects ISO dates and `date,description,reference,amount` headers. Completed periods block later backdated bank journal entries. Bank opening, create/match/unmatch/complete operations are audited. `audit_logs` is exposed through the read-only `/api/audit/logs` route with date, actor/action/record/reason and entity-type filters; the audit center displays event metadata and expandable before/after JSON. Migration 031 seeds a system Auditor role for existing companies with read-only/view permissions; initial company setup creates the same role for new companies. Owner-authorized user endpoints can create only Auditor accounts through this screen, hash their initial passwords, deactivate/reactivate users, revoke active sessions on deactivation, prevent self-deactivation and preserve at least one active owner. Migration 084 seeds Manager and Cashier operational role presets; owners create staff accounts, assign built-in/custom roles and edit custom permission sets from `/admin/roles`. Migrations 084–085 add owner-managed company-name/logo settings and private logo metadata/content consistency checks.

Employee opening data cannot be posted after another employee ledger transaction exists.

`035_financial_year_closures.sql` assigns existing and future journal entries to the year containing their effective date; creates audited year-close records; seeds retained earnings and close numbering; and grants the owner year-close permission. Closing requires the year to have ended, the next consecutive year to be open, and all twelve calendar months to be closed. Income and expense balances are reversed into retained earnings in the next year, leaving asset and liability balances continuous in the ledger. The close journal has a narrow exception to the month lock for its effective date at the new year boundary; all ordinary journal and stock postings remain locked. New postings to a closed year are rejected in `writeJournal`.

Migration 036 creates one immutable, auditable per-account opening-balance snapshot for a financial year. It seeds a baseline for each configured year without a directly preceding year; an audited annual close creates the following year snapshot in the same transaction as the closing journal. Snapshot capture stores the signed debit or credit balance, reason, timestamp, actor and source close. A first or legacy year can capture a reasoned baseline after earlier years have been closed.

Migration 037 backfills opening snapshots for financial years that already had an audited preceding-year close before migration 036 was installed.

Migration 041 adds tenant-scoped vehicle/driver masters and indexed, composite-foreign-key references from loading sessions. Existing loading rows retain their recorded vehicle/driver text snapshots and remain valid without a historical master assignment.

Migration 042 adds audited direct production cost lines and a company-level overhead allocation policy. A database trigger snapshots the current method and rate onto each new batch, so later policy changes affect only new batches.

Migration 043 adds loading, firing, cooling and unloading production stages, stores loaded and process-loss quantities, and maintains an append-only milestone event history. Existing planned/running/completed/cancelled records are backfilled without inventing unavailable intermediate timestamps.

Migration 038 adds tenant-scoped account groups, assigns all existing chart accounts to a matching standard group, marks hard-coded posting accounts as system-managed, and adds owner permissions for chart maintenance. Custom accounts and subgroups can be added with a matching account type; codes and types remain immutable, used ledger accounts cannot be deactivated, and all edits are audited.

Migration 039 adds the owner-only role-management permission. Owners can create custom roles, select action-level permissions, create staff accounts with roles, and reassign roles. The system Owner and read-only Auditor roles are protected; role-manager permission cannot be delegated; read-only roles cannot be combined with operational roles; role and assignment changes are audited, and assignment changes revoke active sessions for the affected user.

## Remaining schema work

Branch access reuses the `branches`, `user_branches`, and `sessions.branch_id` structures from migration 001; no new migration is required for the current access foundation. Owner branch administration and user assignments are audited. Authentication enforces assignments, auto-selects a user's only assigned active branch, and requires users with multiple assigned branches to select one. Existing branch/location dimensions are used to scope stock locations and movements, valuation, stocktakes, expenses, relevant approvals, and branch-tagged audit events. Migration 061 adds branch attribution to production batches and creates a dedicated yard per active branch; new branch creation creates its yard in the same transaction. Production batch lists, costs, milestones and actions respect the active branch, and completed output posts to the matching branch yard. Branchless legacy batches and stock locations remain shared. Production audit events are not yet tagged with their batch branch. Core sales, purchases and accounting records still lack consistent branch attribution, so end-to-end branch isolation and consolidated reporting remain open PRD work.

Location records can be renamed, deactivated and reactivated through the stock-location management endpoint. Deactivation keeps the history intact and is blocked while stock remains, a stocktake is counting or pending, or the company would have no active location. Every change is audited; no schema migration is needed.

The PRD also requires conversion display in operational reports, general stock returns and shipment-linked stock timing, supplier statutory reporting, richer delivery proof and private object storage, payroll statutory rules/paid leave, historic FIFO cost reconstruction and period inventory reconciliation, offline transaction synchronization, external notification delivery, integrations, audit exports and full UAT. Employee/driver/vehicle masters, production milestones, current FIFO workflows, core salary runs, approval workflows and owner in-app alerts are implemented but do not complete these larger areas.



Migration 044 adds chamber section count and location metadata, operational status (available, maintenance, out of service), and an owner-managed status permission. Planned and in-progress batches reserve capacity while active; creation checks the chamber's remaining capacity and matching product unit. Kiln loading rechecks current availability and physical capacity. A zero capacity means no configured limit.

Migration 045 adds FIFO inventory cost layers and per-outbound-movement layer consumption, with explicit valued/unvalued stock movement state. Supplier receipts use actual purchase rates; production completion capitalizes recorded direct batch costs plus the snapshotted overhead policy to saleable output and posts Inventory/COGS journals; transfers carry source FIFO cost. Opening stock and positive adjustments accept user-entered unit costs. The current valuation report separates physical on-hand, valued/unvalued units, and remaining FIFO inventory value. Existing historic movements stay unvalued unless a cost baseline is explicitly recorded; outbounds requiring FIFO cost are blocked when cost layers are insufficient. Returns and reversals require traceable invoice cost. Sales COGS is posted per FIFO cost consumption. Historical-date FIFO valuation and full reconciliation of legacy transactions remain necessary before representing historical P&L/COGS or balance-sheet inventory as complete.



Migration 046 separates Cost of Goods Sold (5200) from ordinary Operating Expenses (5100). New company setup creates account groups before seeded accounts so required group links are present.


Migrations 047–048 add employee pay configuration, monthly proration settings, auditable salary runs and lines, approval and partial payment records. Approving a run debits salary expense (5400) and credits employee advance recovery (1400), deductions payable (2400), and salary payable (2500); payments debit salary payable and credit cash/bank. Statutory withholding remains an explicitly entered deduction amount and must be configured with the business accountant.

Migration 049 adds same-supplier allocation rows for supplier return credits, backfills existing returns against their original bill up to its unpaid balance, and grants the owner permission to apply unused credit to another open bill. Allocation is an audited payable-subledger designation; the original return journal already records the supplier credit, so applying that credit does not create a second accounting entry.


Migration 050 adds company-scoped HSN/SAC product tax profiles with separate sales and purchase rates. Profile selection snapshots rates onto newly created products; transactional documents continue to snapshot product rates, and existing products are not rewritten. Profile creation/status changes are audited and owner-permissioned.

Migration 051 creates product-specific unit conversion definitions with one canonical base unit per product, owner configuration permission, and an audit path. Existing products receive a base-unit mapping. Migration 052 snapshots the quantity entered, unit code and conversion ratio on sales and purchase order lines while retaining the base quantity in the existing quantity column. Migration 053 snapshots production batch planned and kiln-loaded quantities in the chosen product unit; migration 058 preserves entered good/second/broken/rejected output quantities and output unit/factor, while planned, loaded and posted output quantities remain in the product base unit. Migration 054 snapshots purchase receipt entries and converts them to base quantities before updating order receipts, stock and FIFO cost layers; migration 055 corrects legacy receipt snapshots. Migration 056 snapshots invoice and loading quantities, 057 snapshots physical counts and manual adjustments, and 059 snapshots supplier return entries. Sales, purchase order, purchase receipt, batch planning/loading/output, stocktake, manual adjustment, delivery and supplier return entry convert to base quantities and preserve entry-unit snapshots. Stock balance and FIFO valuation screens show base quantities with configured-unit equivalents. Conversion displays in operational reports remain outstanding; opening stock and inter-location transfer entry now accept and preserve configured product units.


Migration 060 adds entered quantity, unit code and conversion snapshots to opening-stock lines, plus entered-unit and base-unit cost snapshots. The existing quantity column remains in product base units. Inter-location transfers now also preserve entered quantity/unit/factor while transfer quantities and paired stock movements remain in base units. Legacy entries are backfilled with each product base unit. Opening costs entered per configured unit are divided by the conversion factor for FIFO valuation; transferred inventory retains the consumed source FIFO cost.


Migration 061 adds a nullable branch foreign key and recent-activity index to production batches. Existing batches remain branchless shared history. It also creates a branch-specific stock yard for each active branch using a stable branch-derived location code. New branch creation adds the same yard atomically with the branch and its audit event.

Migrations 062–065 add branch attribution to sales orders and downstream invoices, returns, receipts, receipt allocations, customer credit applications/refunds, invoice reversals and journal headers. New documents inherit their parent's branch and database triggers reject branch mismatches, including settlement across two explicitly assigned branches; existing history is backfilled when a parent already has branch attribution, while otherwise remaining shared. Sales order/invoice and settlement views and posting routes restrict branch-owned records; invoice stock issues prefer the branch yard when it can fulfill the line and otherwise use the shared Main yard, and returns/reversals restore the original issue location.

Migrations 066–068 add branch attribution to purchase orders, receipts, bills, supplier payments, purchase returns/credit allocations, and loading sessions; journal entries for these sources are tagged from their parent. Child-document triggers enforce parent branch consistency, including supplier-credit allocations across the credit and target bill. Purchase order/bill lists, receipt and payment/return posting, supplier lists/ledger, and loading lists/actions apply branch visibility. Loading creation inherits its invoice branch. Branch-specific stock is preferred for supplier returns, with shared Main used when needed. Migration 067 tags new audit events from their source entity. Branchless historical entries remain shared. `SELECT revert_purchase_loading_branch_scope();` manually reverses migration 066–068, including clearing branch tags from purchase journals, and should only be used as a deliberate rollback; sales branch scope remains independent.

Trial balance, P&L, balance sheet and cash/bank book queries apply the active branch to journal entries and include branchless shared history; branch-specific trial balance does not reuse the company-wide opening snapshot. A smoke check confirms branch A excludes branch B test journals while company-wide results include both. Migration 070 adds branch attribution and branch/date uniqueness to daily cash closes. Expected balances and close approvals are scoped to the selected branch (including legacy branchless shared entries); monthly close requires an approved cash close for each branch with cash activity. `SELECT revert_cash_closing_branch_scope();` manually reverts the addition when same-day closes do not overlap across branches. Migration 071 adds branch foreign keys and branch indexes to bank opening balances and reconciliation periods, replacing the single-company opening constraint with one opening per branch/shared scope. Legacy records stay branchless and shared. A database trigger prevents overlapping reconciliation periods for the same branch (or against a company-wide period) and rejects matching a journal line owned by another branch. Opening journals and reconciliation audit events carry the selected branch. Branch views see their opening plus shared history; company-wide views can inspect branch openings and reconciliations. Month close checks a completed full-month reconciliation for each branch with bank activity. `SELECT revert_bank_reconciliation_branch_scope();` manually rolls back the additions when no company has multiple branch opening balances. Remaining sales detail routes, comprehensive purchase detail/evidence route review, branch-aware production audit history, and consolidated reporting still need full branch scoping. To reverse the sales additions, run `SELECT revert_sales_settlement_branch_scope();` followed by `SELECT revert_sales_branch_scope();`; this removes the new attribution data and should only be done as a deliberate database rollback.

Customer receivables balance summaries filter the journal lines themselves by active branch, and customer/broker ledgers include only branch-owned and shared legacy journals. Broker commission lists/actions, accrual and payout journals, and audit events follow the source invoice branch. Customer-specific product rates remain company-wide configuration; their activate/deactivate query uses only columns present in the rate table. This follow-up is build-checked and still requires authenticated branch-to-branch UAT.

Migration `080_owner_notifications.sql` adds per-user notification inbox rows keyed to their source audit event. An audit trigger creates bilingual owner alerts for invoice posting, receipts, expense approval requests, loading/verification/mismatch, dispatch/delivery and completed production. Notifications preserve the source branch, are generated in the same transaction as their audit event, and cannot be read or marked by a different user/company; active-branch users see shared and matching-branch events. The inbox is capped at the 100 most recent items and supports idempotent read/read-all actions. Live API/database verification exercised trigger delivery and read state with temporary audit events, then removed the test user and events.

Migration `081_notification_preferences.sql` adds company-wide, owner-configurable on/off preferences per supported alert event. The audit trigger checks the preference before creating owner inbox rows; switching a category off affects future events and does not delete existing notifications. The signed-in API exposes preferences only to owners, writes changes to `company_settings`, and audits each update. Live verification confirmed an enabled event is delivered, a disabled event is suppressed, and the previous preference map is restored afterward.

Migration 072 adds `transaction_idempotency_keys`, scoped by company, user, operation, and client key. Customer receipt posting, sales order creation, invoice posting/reversal, sales returns, customer-credit settlements, on-account receipt allocations, broker commission approval/payouts, purchase order creation/receipts/bills/payments/returns/credit allocations, expense posting/approval/rejection, stock openings/transfers/adjustments, stocktake creation/submission/review, employee opening balances/advances/repayments, payroll run creation/approval/payment, daily cash-close recording/approval, bank opening balances/reconciliation creation/completion, financial-year creation/opening snapshots/close, monthly period close, loading creation/photos/quantity verification, delivery mismatch approval/dispatch/completion, and production batch creation/start/cancel/milestones/completion/cost postings claim the key, compare a canonical request hash, and persist the success status/body in the same PostgreSQL transaction as the posting. Identical retries replay the stored response; reusing a key with changed details returns a conflict. Relevant screens retain generated keys in session storage across failed network attempts and clear them after success. The migration can be reverted with `SELECT revert_transaction_idempotency();` only when no idempotency rows exist. This coverage applies to the listed endpoints; it does not establish global idempotency for every financial, inventory, setup, or administrative write.

Migration 073 adds `purchase_return_refunds` for cash/bank refunds against unused supplier return credit. Each refund inherits and is database-checked against its return’s branch, is permission-controlled and audited, uses an idempotency key, and posts a balanced supplier-payable/cash-or-bank journal in the same transaction. The available credit query subtracts both bill allocations and prior refunds.

Migration 074 (`074_inventory_valuation_reconciliation.sql`) expands allowed cost-layer sources for valuation reconciliation and creates reconciliation headers/lines, branch/location consistency enforcement, an owner permission, and document numbering. The API computes unvalued current stock at posting time, then creates FIFO layers and a same-day inventory/opening-equity journal in one idempotent transaction. It does not backfill historical cost of sales.

Migration 075 (`075_inventory_batch_provenance.sql`) adds a nullable production-batch origin to FIFO cost layers, backfills only production layers whose source batch is verifiable, and indexes remaining batch-linked layers. New production output layers carry the batch ID; transfers and damage reclassification split destination layers by consumed source layer to preserve batch, quantity, and unit cost. Existing transfer/damage and batch-less opening/adjustment history remains unassigned. The stock report exposes remaining valued FIFO layers by product, quality, location, batch, quantity, unit cost, and value; it does not reconstruct past stock movements by batch or fabricate origins for legacy rows.

Migration 076 (`076_credit_sale_approvals.sql`) adds a pending-approval and rejected state for sales orders, stores approver, reason, timestamp and explicit credit-limit override, and grants the owner the `sales.approve` permission. Orders that exceed a customer's configured credit limit remain uninvoiceable until approval; approval/rejection is idempotent, branch-scoped, and audited.

Migration 077 (`077_employee_advance_approval.sql`) adds pending/rejected employee advance states and approver/decision audit fields, plus the `employees.advance.approve` permission granted to owners. Advance requests created by a user without approval permission remain unposted in the approval queue; approval creates the receivable and cash/bank journal atomically, while rejection has no journal effect. Amount-based approval thresholds are not configured yet.

Migration 078 (`078_employee_advance_approval_limits.sql`) grants owners the `employees.advance.configure` permission. The company-level threshold is stored in `company_settings` as `employee_advance_approval_threshold`; a null threshold disables amount-based routing, while users without the approve permission still submit requests for review.

Migration 079 (`079_manual_journal_approvals.sql`) adds manual journal request headers and debit/credit lines, request numbering, branch ownership, pending/posted/rejected decisions, journal-entry linkage, indexes, and `accounting.journal.create` / `accounting.journal.approve` permissions. Requests require balanced lines and a business reason. Approval posts through the normal journal writer, so financial-year, closed-period, cash-close, bank-reconciliation, and ledger checks still apply. The requester cannot approve their own request. Rejection creates no ledger entry.

The sales register endpoint `GET /api/reports/sales-register` reads posted invoice lines with invoice/customer/broker snapshots, receipt and on-account allocation totals, return totals, payment status, branch scope, and invoice-line entered/base-unit quantities. It is read-only and capped at 10,000 lines; no migration is needed because it uses existing sales tables and unit snapshots.


Migration 086 (`086_production_cost_work_in_process.sql`) adds a funding account snapshot to production cost lines and seeds the Production WIP asset account (1350). A cost entry posts a balanced debit to WIP and credit to the chosen cash, bank or accounts-payable account. Batch completion transfers direct costs from WIP into finished inventory; allocated overhead is reclassified from operating expenses. Older unfinished batches with unjournaled cost lines are posted during completion. Historical completed-batch journal reclassification and period inventory reconciliation remain accountant-controlled follow-up work.

Migration 087 seeds payroll liability and salary expense accounts for companies that already exist. First-time setup also creates those accounts, since migrations can run before the first company exists.

Migration 088 adds a false-by-default `permissions.manager_default` flag and marks the built-in operational/reporting permissions allowed for the Manager preset. Existing Manager roles lose permissions outside that allowlist. New permissions are excluded until explicitly approved for the preset; Owners can still construct deliberately scoped custom roles. Invoice reversal additionally checks for an Owner role at the API boundary. The reversal restores FIFO-valued stock and posts the counter-journal atomically.
